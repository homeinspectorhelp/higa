## 1. The Big Picture — One Integrated Ecosystem
HIH is **not three separate companies** — it's a vertically integrated growth system for home inspectors. Each company solves a different part of the same inspector's problem:

| Company | Problem it solves |
|---|---|
| **HIH** (marketing) | Get the inspector **found** — website, GBP, newsletter, **paid ads** |
| **Outcrop Inspector** (sister co.) | Get leads **answered & booked** — AI call answering / scheduling (launches **June 29**) |
| **Inspector Playbook** (Higa / Growth Academy) | Teach the inspector how to **convert & grow** — training + systems |
| **GHL CRM** (the anchor) | **Hold it all together** — client history, follow-up, referrals, pipeline |

**No competitor offers all three integrated.** That's the moat. Positioning: *"the only complete growth system built exclusively for home inspectors."*

## 2. The CRM Is the Strategic Anchor
A GHL-based CRM provided through Inspector Playbook creates **operating-system-level stickiness**: when an inspector runs his whole business inside a CRM HIH configured, leaving means replacing his entire operating system — not just swapping an agency. It also builds a **proprietary data layer** on inspector performance that sharpens HIH's marketing, Outcrop's call handling, and Inspector Playbook's training over time.

## 3. The Category-Defining Vision — Property Consultant
The real estate commission structure is heading toward a breaking point (NAR settlement raised cost-awareness). As AI makes transactions more navigable, a gap opens for a credible, **commission-conflict-free** property expert. A trained home inspector fills it — the **independent property consultant** consumers can turn to at any stage (buying, pre-listing/FSBO, ongoing maintenance). Inspector Playbook trains for it; HIH makes them findable; Outcrop answers; the CRM manages the long relationship. This is a far larger market than "home inspection marketing."

## 4. The Google Ads Product (immediate, actionable)
**One goal only:** a phone call or booked appointment. Everything flows backward from that single conversion.

**Campaign architecture (single-inspector market):**
- **Campaign 1 — Branded Search:** protect the inspector's name; cheap, high-converting, always on.
- **Campaign 2 — High-Intent Buyer Search (revenue engine):** "home inspection [city]", "home inspector near me", "book/schedule home inspection", "certified home inspector [city]". **Exact + Phrase match only — never broad** (broad match bleeds budget on research queries; likely the cause of the underperforming account Beth found).
- **Campaign 3 — Pre-Listing / Seller (optional growth):** "pre-listing inspection [city]", "sell my home inspection". Low volume, near-zero competition, high seller anxiety.

**Ad copy:** urgency → trust signal → local specificity. Sell the *outcome* (confidence, speed, handled), not the inspection. Extensions non-negotiable (call, location/GBP, sitelinks: Schedule Now / Radon / Pre-Listing / About).

**Landing page:** dedicated page (GHL or HIH site), headline mirrors the search term, social proof above the fold, booking widget/short form + click-to-call visible without scrolling, **no nav, no distractions**. Never send paid traffic to the homepage. (Strong argument for the full HIH website service — HIH controls the page = controls Quality Score.)

**Geo/budget:** target the real service radius (20–35 mi), not the whole metro. Honest spend: **$400–$800/mo** most markets; **$1,000–$1,500** high-competition metros (Atlanta, Dallas, Phoenix). Pass-through spend — the inspector owns and controls his budget.

**Negative keywords (built at setup, maintained monthly):** DIY/checklist/tips, "what does a home inspector look for", inspector training/school, software, free, warranty, commercial, vehicle, competitor brands.

**Reporting (one page, outcome-focused):** calls, bookings, cost per lead, top keywords, one recommendation for next 30 days. *"I spent $600 and got 14 calls"* — make it a business conversation, not a marketing one.

## 5. Pricing
- **Setup fee:** $300–$500 one-time (campaign build, keyword research, ad copy, negatives, conversion tracking, landing page, baseline report). **Don't waive it** — covers Beth's heaviest time, creates commitment, filters non-serious inspectors.
- **Monthly management:** $250–$350. **Published price: $297/mo** (signals a productized service; room to bundle-discount).
- **Bundle play:** existing full-stack clients (~$1,000+/mo with HIH) get ~$50 off ads. New prospects can enter *through* ads, then upsell the other services.
- **ROI framing:** ~$297 + ~$500 spend ≈ $800/mo; at $400/inspection & 40% close, **~5 calls/mo to break even** (≈1 qualified call/week). Sell the break-even math, not the fee.

## 6. What Keeps It Profitable & Scalable
1. **Call tracking is non-negotiable** — Google call tracking + GHL call tracking (records + ties to CRM). No attribution = can't optimize or defend the fee.
2. **Seasonality** — demand tracks real estate (peaks spring/summer, dips winter). Set expectations at onboarding; recommend seasonal spend adjustments.
3. **Speed to lead is THE variable** — 5-min response converts far better. Outcrop / GHL auto-text within 60s keeps leads warm. (This is why Outcrop + ads are inseparable.)
4. **Quality Score** — controls CPC. HIH-built landing page = control over one of the three factors. Flag any keyword < 6 monthly.
5. **Competitor response** — bids rise 24–48h after launch; first 60–90 days are data→refine→Smart Bidding. Tell the inspector upfront so month-1 doesn't trigger panic.
6. **Match-type trap** — SOP rule: **never follow Google's auto-recommendations without human review**; audit match types monthly; avoid Performance Max for local service until data justifies it.
7. **Read-only account access** for inspectors who want to "log in" — they see everything, change nothing.
8. **Service agreement clause** — define on cancellation who owns campaign data, negatives, conversion tracking, landing page (account under inspector's Google login; architecture/copy/strategy are HIH's).
9. **Retention lever** — ads + website + GBP together = real switching cost. The product is most powerful as part of the full stack.

## 7. Cross-Company Customer Journey & LTV
Every lead becomes a lead for more than one company — and **each company creates the problem awareness that makes the next one obvious**: more ads → more calls → Outcrop; more growth → ceiling → Inspector Playbook. A single-service client ≈ $800/mo; full ecosystem (HIH + Outcrop ~$200/mo + Inspector Playbook ~$500) can be **~$1,500/mo per relationship** — which also lets HIH spend more aggressively to acquire each inspector. Inspector Playbook's audience is a warm, near-free prospect list for HIH + Outcrop.
