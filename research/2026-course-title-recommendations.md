## HIGH-PRIORITY COURSES (Market Demand + Revenue Impact)

### 1. From One-Time Jobs to Recurring Revenue: Building Inspection Service Agreements (ISAs)
**Description:** Teach inspectors how to shift from transactional inspection models to recurring revenue through maintenance plans and ISAs.  
**Based on:** Business Model Evolution; Pricing & Revenue; Client Experience & Retention

### 2. The $847 Ticket: How to Bundle Ancillary Services for 2-3x Revenue Growth
**Description:** Show inspectors how to strategically add radon, mold, sewer scope, and pool inspections to increase average revenue per inspection.  
**Based on:** Business Model Evolution; Pricing & Revenue; Opportunities

### 3. Escape Realtor Dependency: Direct-to-Consumer Marketing for Home Inspectors
**Description:** Train inspectors to reduce Realtor reliance by building direct buyer channels through Google, social media, email, and video marketing.  
**Based on:** Marketing & Lead Generation; Challenges; Opportunities

### 4. Short-Form Video for Inspectors: TikTok, Reels & YouTube Shorts That Convert
**Description:** Teach inspectors how to create and deploy short-form video content to drive 55% more inbound inquiries.  
**Based on:** Marketing & Lead Generation

### 5. Value-Based Pricing for Inspectors: Charge 22% More and Get More Bookings
**Description:** Train inspectors to communicate value and implement tiered pricing (Good/Better/Best) instead of flat fees.  
**Based on:** Pricing & Revenue
